Crowdsurf pulls free public marine data — NOAA buoys, marine forecasts, tides, wind — for a specific surf break and shows it plainly, no paywall. It then layers in short eyewitness reports from surfers standing on the sand. Same swell, every spot breaks differently, and only people at the beach can see that.
