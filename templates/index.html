Ask the water.

Crowdsurf pulls live public marine data — NOAA buoys, NWS forecasts and alerts, CO-OPS tides, SPC outlooks, Open-Meteo swell/wind, RainViewer radar — for a specific surf break and shows it plainly, no paywall. It then layers in short eyewitness reports from surfers standing on the sand. Same swell, every spot breaks differently, and only people at the beach can see that.
